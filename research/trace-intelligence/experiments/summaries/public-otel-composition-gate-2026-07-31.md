# Public OTel composition gate (2026-07-31)

The public OTel composition combines the Signals-style selector arms with the
governed hypothesis-policy lifecycle. The lifecycle mechanics are present, but
promotion is **deferred**: selector behavior disagrees across workload strata
and there is no independent human outcome or changed-system causal result.

This is the intended fail-closed behavior. A positive selector slice plus a
working proposal/release pipeline is not enough to ship automatic proposals.
The system may expose review candidates and evidence packets, but must not claim
diagnosis, enterprise skill inference, or autonomous memory/skill improvement.
