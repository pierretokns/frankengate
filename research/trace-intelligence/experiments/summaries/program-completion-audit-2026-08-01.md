This is a requirement-level audit, not a progress score. The machine-readable
result identifies the authoritative evidence for each objective and records
whether it is proven, bounded, local-only, externally blocked, or complete.

mechanics, governed PostgreSQL mechanics, issue/bead recording, and branch

those missing external or causal requirements.
