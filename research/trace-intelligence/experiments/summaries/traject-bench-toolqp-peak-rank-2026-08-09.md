# ToolQP peak-rank aggregation reproduction (2026-08-09)

The official [TOOLQP paper](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.2090/)
defines an iterative planner over a retriever and aggregates each tool by its
best rank across retrieval turns. Its full method also trains on synthetic
trajectories and applies SFT plus RLVR/GRPO; this experiment does **not**
reproduce that training.

## What was reproduced

- Reused the eight public TRAJECT-Bench cases and the already recorded planner
  outputs from the earlier no-target-append probe.
- Kept the planner blind to gold target tools and tool outputs.
- Retrieved the original query plus three planner queries with the same
  deterministic domain-scoped name-token retriever.
- Compared the original baseline, the existing union ranker, and ToolQP-style
  peak-rank aggregation with top-5 feedback per turn.

## Result

| Arm | Candidate coverage in top-16 | MRR | Recall@1 | Recall@5 | Recall@10 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Baseline | 0.666667 | 0.348333 | 0.041667 | 0.250000 | 0.541667 |
| Query union | 0.750000 | **0.562500** | **0.083333** | **0.458333** | **0.625000** |
| Peak rank | 0.750000 | 0.419118 | 0.041667 | 0.375000 | 0.541667 |

On this eight-case public probe, peak-rank preserved the query-planning
coverage gain and improved Recall@5 over baseline, but underperformed the
simpler union ranker on MRR, Recall@1, and Recall@10. This isolates a useful
boundary: query generation and aggregation are separate hypotheses; the paper's
headline gains cannot be attributed to peak-rank alone.

## Interpretation and limits

The result is not a ToolQP model reproduction, RLVR result, or enterprise
artifact result. The planner outputs were generated by a frontier model, the
retriever was lexical rather than dense, and the benchmark targets are public
synthetic tool lists. The next fair test is to freeze candidate coverage and
compare peak-rank, union, and learned aggregation on a larger family-disjoint
cohort, then measure independent tool execution and governed artifact replay.
