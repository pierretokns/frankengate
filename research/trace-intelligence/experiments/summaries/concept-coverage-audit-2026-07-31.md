# Named-concept coverage audit (2026-07-31)

This checkpoint audits every major concept named in the research program against a concrete artifact. It prevents the report from implying that a literature review is equivalent to an empirical validation.

## Results

The machine-readable ledger is [`experiments/results/concept-coverage-audit-2026-07-31.json`](../results/concept-coverage-audit-2026-07-31.json). The strongest evidence is currently for ATIF/OTel projections, Signals selection, RL retention, NL2SQL mechanics, and the governed proposal lifecycle. Memory systems, diagnosis, skill learning, and retrieval backends remain bounded or deferred because causal held-out outcomes, human labels, or deployment evidence are absent.

The architecture therefore remains intentionally small: a governed canonical event store, OTel/ATIF projections, structured evidence packets, and controlled proposal/eval promotion. Graphs, custom embeddings, additional vector databases, and autonomous skill release are not admitted by citation alone.

## Completion boundary

The audit does **not** claim that the full enterprise research objective is complete. The decisive missing experiment is a family-disjoint evaluation on real or authorized traces with independent human adjudication and a changed-system outcome. CMU remains access-gated; no raw CMU data was downloaded.
