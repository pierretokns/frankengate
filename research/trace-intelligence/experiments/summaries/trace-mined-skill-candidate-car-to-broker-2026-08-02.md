# True trace-mined candidate transfer: car to broker

The candidate artifact was mined deterministically from the external 18-file
car schema-injected audit directory. The miner observed 18/18 files without a
`describe_schema` call and 14/18 files with policy denials, then emitted a
generic schema/identifier/terminal procedure. The committed artifact contains
only its source-directory digest, aggregate signatures, candidate hash, and
promotion=false; raw prompts, SQL, rows, and messages remain external.

That sealed candidate was injected only into the trace-mined arm of a new six-
task broker fold. No-skill and placebo controls were unchanged. Results were
identical to the fixed-candidate transfer: no-skill 0/6, placebo 1/6 correct,
and the genuinely trace-mined candidate 0/6. Fresh semantic and raw-security
verification passed all 18 episodes. This is a real train-on-car/test-on-
broker transfer null for the mined candidate, not a skill-promotion result.
