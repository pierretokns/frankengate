## The two artifact results must be read together

The natural held-out cohort retrieved zero semantically correct artifacts because
the source pool had almost no compatible procedures. The controlled upper-bound
cohort deliberately created one known-compatible artifact per target and
recovered it at 20/20 for lexical, dense, and hybrid retrieval. Therefore:

- “nearest stored SQL always transfers” is disproven;
- “artifact reuse never works” is also disproven;
- the missing variable is source-pool coverage and semantic equivalence, not
  merely the choice of embedding model.

The positive control is [validated-artifact-shared-intent-2026-08-04](validated-artifact-shared-intent-2026-08-04.md); the natural null is
[validated-artifact-retrieval-comparison-2026-08-04](validated-artifact-retrieval-comparison-2026-08-04.md).

## Promotion decision

No custom embedding, automatically generated skill, memory, or cross-user
recommendation should be promoted from the current evidence. The safe current
product boundary is a provenance-aware candidate queue with:

1. exact identifier and scope filtering;
2. lexical/dense/hybrid candidate retrieval;
3. explicit NIL and uncertainty handling;
4. independent replay and authority checks;
5. human review before release; and
6. changed-system validation before reuse.

## Decisive next study

The P0 study remains a consented 20–40 task cohort with at least two users or
teams, repeated intents, parameterized artifacts, schema/time/project holdouts,
wrong-system and stale negatives, independent semantic labels, and terminal
replay outcomes. Pre-register no-artifact/regenerate, exact, lexical, dense,
identifier-aware, composed-subplan, and frontier arms. Report retrieval,
artifact validity, changed-system success, latency/cost, and abstention as
separate endpoints.

This audit does not mark the objective complete; the P0 cohort and reviewed
held-out alias/NIL labels remain open.
